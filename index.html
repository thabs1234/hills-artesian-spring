FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common trade questions.

What makes Hills Artesian Spring Water different from other bottled water?

Hills Artesian Spring Water is sourced from a uniquely protected volcanic aquifer beneath the Sabi Sand region of Mpumalanga, South Africa. The water is naturally filtered through ancient basalt rock, giving it a naturally pure profile (pH 7.0 for still, pH 4.9 for sparkling) and a smooth, clean taste with no added minerals or chemicals.

Is the water naturally still and sparkling, or is it enhanced?

The still and sparkling are both naturally sourced from the same volcanic aquifer. The sparkling version naturally carbonates at depth under pressure, creating a naturally crisp, effervescent taste. No carbonation is added artificially.

Where is Hills Artesian Spring Water bottled?

All Hills Artesian Spring Water is bottled at the source in Malelane, Mpumalanga, South Africa. The bottling facility is located directly above the aquifer, ensuring minimal handling and maximum freshness.
